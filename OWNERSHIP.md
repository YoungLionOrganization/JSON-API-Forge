## Can the project later become company-owned?

Yes, in principle. If Cavanşir Qurbanzadə later forms a company, the relevant copyrights and other project rights can be transferred or assigned to that company through a proper written intellectual-property assignment or other legally effective transfer under the applicable law.

The repository can also be transferred from the personal GitHub account to a company/organization account. A repository transfer by itself is not a substitute for the legal transfer of copyright or other intellectual-property rights; the legal ownership documents and the GitHub repository ownership should be kept consistent.

## What should be transferred to a future company?

A formal transfer should consider, as applicable:

- copyright in the original JSON API Forge source code, documentation, schemas, examples, and project materials;
- rights to license and relicense the project;
- contractual rights received through contributor agreements;
- trademarks, logos, domains, package names, and other branding rights, if any;
- repository, release, signing-key, domain, package-registry, and infrastructure control;
- commercial/OEM/redistribution agreements already entered into by the owner.

Because legal assignment formalities vary by jurisdiction, the actual transfer should be documented in writing and reviewed under the law governing the owner/company.

## What happens to existing users after a transfer?

A lawful ownership transfer does not normally erase licenses already validly granted to existing recipients. A user who received a version under a particular license continues to have the permissions of that license, subject to its terms. The successor company can become the Licensor for rights that were validly transferred and can choose licensing terms for future releases to the extent it owns or controls the necessary rights.

## Why the CLA matters

External contributors retain copyright in their contributions under the current CLA. However, the CLA grants the Project Owner broad, perpetual, sublicensable, **transferable and assignable** rights, including relicensing rights. This is intended to let those contractual rights move with JSON API Forge to a future company or other lawful successor without requiring every historical contributor to sign a new agreement merely because the project owner changes.

The CLA is not intended to transfer a contributor's underlying copyright ownership unless a separate written agreement explicitly does so.

## After a company transfer

For the first release after a transfer, update at least:

1. `LICENSE` — identify the new lawful Licensor/copyright owner as appropriate.
2. `NOTICE.md` and `AUTHORS.md` — record the original creator and current owner accurately.
3. `OWNERSHIP.md` — record the effective transfer and successor entity.
4. `GOVERNANCE.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `SUPPORT.md`, and `BRAND.md` — update authority/contact references.
5. `.github/CODEOWNERS` — point to the organization/team that maintains the project.
6. Canonical repository links if the repository moves to an organization.
7. Any copyright registration, trademark, domain, signing-key, package-registry, or commercial contract records that apply.

For a commercially important transfer, use a qualified intellectual-property lawyer to prepare or review the assignment documents.
